Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to the Local Community Event Portal Help Center. Here are general answers to help you navigate and interact with local services.

How do I register for an event?

Scroll down to the "Event Registration" section on the home page, fill in your Name, Email, Phone Number, select your preferred Event Program, select a date, and click the "Confirm Registration" button.

Why does the page ask to save preferences?

Clicking "Save Favorite" saves your selected Event Program in the browser's localStorage and sessionStorage. When you visit the portal next time, your preferred event program will be pre-selected automatically.

How can I clear my saved preferences?

Simply click the "Reset Favorites" button next to the "Save Favorite" button on the registration form. This will clear all stored caching fields instantly.

Why does the portal request my location?

The "Find Nearby Events" function uses high-accuracy HTML5 Geolocation to find your current coordinate numbers to locate nearest city council resources and halls. If you deny the permission, you can still view all list programs manually.
