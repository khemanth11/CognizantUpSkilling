Vanilla/jQuery vs. Frameworks

Why move from jQuery to React/Vue?
  • Declarative UI: Frameworks sync UI state automatically, avoiding tedious imperative DOM edits.
  • Componentization: Reusable chunks make large codebases maintainable.
  • Virtual DOM: Highly optimized updates prevent expensive full-reflow browser tasks.

Community News Bulletin

Summer Programs Launched

The city council has officially approved funding for our new summer recreation programs. This includes free arts classes, outdoor swimming safety workshops, and local environment cleanups. Make sure to check the event calendar regularly as new dates are added weekly. Volunteers are always welcome!

Renovation of Civic Hall

Starting next month, the main hall of the civic center will undergo full renovations. Modern energy-efficient lighting, enhanced acoustics, and improved accessibility features will be installed. All town halls will be moved to the community center gallery temporarily during this period.
